How It Works

✓ Sign in with Google for full access

Signing in unlocks everything Court Manager has to offer:

  • Personal match history — view your record across all past sessions
  • Saved preferences — your name, skill level, and partners are remembered
  • Create private sessions — for league nights or reserved courts
  • Become a session admin — manage courts and players for your private sessions

⚡ Quick Play

No sign-in needed. Tap Quick Play, pick a format — Open Play, Round Robin, or Tournament — and add your players' names. No skill levels or in-session settings to manage, and no one else can join. Drag or tap a player onto Sitting Out to bench them, or onto Leave Session to remove them, at any time. Everything stays on your device — nothing is shared online.

🏓 Score Keeper

Just need to track serve and score for one match? Tap Score Keeper Only on this screen for a standalone scorer — no sign-in, no session, nothing saved. Inside a session, tap Score Keeper on an active court to track serve and score live instead of entering a score manually; finishing the game records the result to history and returns players to the queue automatically.

🔒 Private Sessions

Sign in with Google to create a private session for your group. Share the link or session code with friends — they can join without signing in. Perfect for reserved court nights or league play.

🖥️ On Tablet & Desktop

The queue stays pinned on the left so you can always see who's waiting, while court cards fill the space to the right — two columns on tablets, three across on wider screens or landscape — growing automatically as you add more courts.

🎾 Confirming & Ending a Match (Open Play)

An auto-filled or drag-filled court starts out Proposed — adjust who's on it by dragging or tapping players between the queue and the court, then tap Confirm. Nothing is final until then, and adjusting or cancelling a proposal never costs anyone their place in line — tap ↩ Undo above the queue to step back through adjustments. Once confirmed, tap Start on the court card to start the match timer.

When a match ends, choose Return All 4 (send everyone back to the queue), Keep Winners (needs a score), or Keep Last-Joined (once a court has had a pair carried over before) to decide who stays on for the next match.

⚙️ Updating Your Info

Once in a session, open the Settings tab to update your display name and skill level at any time.

🤝 Adding Partners

Once you're in a session, open the Settings tab to link yourself with another player you'd like to play alongside. Partners are kept together on the same side of the court when courts are assigned.

In Open Play, you can add or remove a partner at any time — the change takes effect the next time courts are assigned. In Round Robin and Tournament, partners must be set while the roster is still being built; once locked, teams are fixed for the rest of the session.

📋 Match History

The History tab shows all completed matches for the current session — courts, opponents, and scores. Signed-in players can also view their personal match history across all past sessions. In Open Play, tap + Log Past Match there to add a match your group already played before opening the app.
