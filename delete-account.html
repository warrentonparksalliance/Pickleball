Court Manager — Delete Your Account & Data

Court Manager is developed and maintained by Warrenton Parks Alliance

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to brooke@warrentonparksalliance.com from the email address associated with your Court Manager account (the one you used to sign in with Google).
  2. Use the subject line "Court Manager Account Deletion Request".
  3. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.
Email to request deletion

What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently delete:

  • Your name and email address associated with your Google Sign-In
  • Your personal session and match history
  • Any player profile data tied to your account

What may be retained

We may retain de-identified, aggregated statistics (for example, total sessions played at a given park) that can no longer be linked back to you. This aggregated data is used only for park usage reporting to Warrenton Parks Alliance's board and grant funders, and is retained indefinitely since it does not identify any individual.

Retention period

Personal data is deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request. Aggregated, de-identified data (described above) has no fixed retention period.
