Court Manager — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Court Manager is provided by Warrenton Parks Alliance, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to keeping Warrenton's parks and trails thriving.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, we store your name and email so you can rejoin sessions and see your match history. Session data (players, scores, court assignments) is stored in our database (Firebase) to run the app's core features.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your information with third parties except as required to operate the app (e.g. our hosting/database provider, Google/Firebase).

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing brooke@warrentonparksalliance.com.
